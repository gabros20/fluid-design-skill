01 — Impasto

Three flours, a lot of water.

Eighty percent hydration is what makes pinsa crisp outside and cloud-soft inside. It is also why the dough cannot be rushed.

02 — Riposo

Seventy-two hours, cold.

The rest does the work a mixer cannot: it breaks the starches down, so the crust is lighter and kinder to eat.

03 — Forno

Ninety seconds of fire.

Stone at four hundred and fifty degrees. The edges blister, the middle stays tender, and it goes straight to your table.
